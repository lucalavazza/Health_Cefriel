
=== SCM-Oriented Interpretation ===


RMSE — Root Mean Squared Error
|___ Lower is better, Best interpreted relative to the scale of the variable
|___ RMSE ≈ 0 → very accurate equation, Large RMSE does not imply causal incorrectness; it may indicate high intrinsic noise
MAE — Mean Absolute Error
|___ Lower is better, More robust to outliers than RMSE
|___ MAE < RMSE → occasional large errors are present, MAE ≈ RMSE → errors are fairly uniform
R² — Coefficient of Determination
|___ Higher is better, Bounded roughly between 0 and 1 (can be negative in poor models)
|___ R² ≥ 0.75 → strong structural determination by parents, 0.30 ≤ R² < 0.75 → partial determination; omitted causes likely, R² < 0.30 → noise-dominated equation

Key takeaway
RMSE / MAE tell how wrong predictions are.
R² tells how structurally informative the causal parents are.
